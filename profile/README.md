# WEFA Labs

WEFA (Water, Earth, Fire, Air) makes stories, games and tools that connect people to nature and to each other. WEFA Labs is the branch of WEFA that builds them, and this organization holds the code.

## What we're building

**[Critter Crumbs](https://github.com/wefa-labs/critter-crumbs)** is a private way for a group to share a budget or recognise contributions. Everyone gets the same handful of crumbs, and every scatter stays encrypted until the feast opens. It has been a testnet beta at [crittercrumbs.app](https://www.crittercrumbs.app) since October 2026.

**The WEFA app** is a game for young players that turns real plant care into play. You look after a plant to earn energy, raise an elemental creature with it, then use the creature in board games, in augmented reality where your phone supports it. It is an early prototype, and its repository is private for now.

## Earlier work

[OpenAR](https://github.com/wefa-labs/openar) (2023, archived): augmented reality tile games, starting with tic-tac-toe and checkers, built on the MUD engine at the Autonomous Worlds hackathon.

## How WEFA is organized

WEFA has four branches, one for each element:

| Element | Branch | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Water | Studio | Story, creatures, comics, film and music |
| Earth | Labs | Software, hardware and research. This organization. |
| Fire | Growth | Brand, partnerships, education and community |
| Air | World | Places and events where people can step into WEFA |

## How we share our work

Our open-source code is MIT licensed. WEFA's characters, artwork, audio, creature generators and branding are reserved, so each repository's licence file says exactly what it covers. See [Critter Crumbs' licence and reuse notes](https://github.com/wefa-labs/critter-crumbs#licence-and-reuse) for the current example.
